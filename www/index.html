Sextant Trainer — Fully Offline
This app is fully offline. All ephemeris computations, sight reductions, corrections and the fix are performed in your browser from pure maths. No internet connection or almanac file is required.
Midday fit (zenith-distance)
The Midday tab fits the parabola z² = A + B·t + C·t² (z = zenith distance, t = minutes from planned LAN) across the ticked Sun sightings within ±3 hours of LAN. The vertex gives the true noon time (→ longitude) and z at minimum (→ latitude). A quality check reports whether the sightings span noon, how much time they cover, and the scatter. When enabled in Setup, the midday fix is combined into the main fix, weighted by its uncertainty.
Direct fix (two-circle intersection)
The Fix tab also shows the Direct fix: the intersection of the two circles of equal altitude around each ticked sight's GP, with no assumed position. Uses the standard Van Allen solution. Two intersections exist; the one nearer the AP is chosen. Only available when exactly two stationary sightings are ticked — for a moving platform use the running fix.
Pub. 229 second-difference
Tables mode has an optional "d on next row" field. When filled in, the Hc correction includes the second-difference term 0.5·f·(f−1)·(d_next − d_current), applied after the linear d × ΔDec. Use when the Hc is above ~60° and the accuracy justifies it.
Running fix — overview
A running fix combines two or more sights of the same body taken at different times. Earlier lines are advanced along the vessel's track by speed × elapsed time, and the fix is where the advanced lines cross.
Running fix — how to use
1. Tick Running fix session, enter track and speed.
2. Take sights — each Save joins the session.
3. Untick when stopped to freeze the DR advance.
4. On the Fix tab, tick sightings from one session for a running fix, or stationary sightings for a stationary fix.

Bottom line
Fully offline, good to sail with now and for the next 20–30 years at current accuracy, usable to about 2200 (Sun/Moon/stars).
